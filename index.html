THE STARTING POINT

Your money. Your assumptions.

Use your available bank and cash balances. Don’t include unused credit or income that hasn’t arrived.

Essentials are a daily estimate for food, transport and similar spending. Don’t also add the same costs as bills. The buffer is a target, not a separate deduction. Refresh your balance regularly; daily allowances do not automatically change it.

Changing currency only relabels amounts, it does not convert them. One currency per plan.

YOU’RE IN CONTROL

A private place to plan.

Your plan is saved in this browser on this device. No account, trackers, AI service or bank access.

On the public demo, GitHub serves the page and may log connection details such as your IP address. The app does not send your entered amounts to the host. Local and online plans stay separate; transfer them with a backup.

Browser storage is not an encrypted vault. Anyone using this browser profile may access it. Clearing browser data can erase your plan. Pages hosted under the same domain share browser storage; do not treat this demo as a secure financial vault.

Backups contain financial amounts in readable JSON. Store them privately.
